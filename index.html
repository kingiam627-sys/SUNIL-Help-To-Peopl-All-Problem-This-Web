🏃 Weight-Loss Tips

  • Reduce frequent sugary drinks and highly processed snacks.
  • Eat vegetables, whole fruits, whole grains and protein foods regularly.
  • Work toward 150 minutes/week of moderate activity if safe for you.
  • Track your weight and habits consistently.
  • Sleep and stress management matter too.
Don't use crash diets or extreme restrictions.
This is educational, not medical advice. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. For pregnancy/breastfeeding, age under 18, medical conditions, medicines affecting weight, or eating-disorder history, use professional guidance.
